Before the trade, not after

No trade goes in without a card. It takes about a minute and it is mostly tapping — it works out the dollars, checks our limits, and tells you whether it needs 3 of 5 or 4 of 5.

  • One block for the chatThe card, with the WInS note inside it. Post it as a new message and the thread starts there.
  • A trade IDLike 0921-MSFT. The same ID goes on the chat card, the WInS note and Federico's row, so the three can be matched up in October.
  • And later, the rowOnce it fills, come back and pick “it went through” — three numbers and it gives Federico his cells.
