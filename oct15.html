Why we are not trading our way out of this

Because we are one session into a six-week portfolio, because an equity round trip costs 0.60% of a position before it earns anything, and because the trades are graded as decisions, not as profit. A trade with no written reason costs us twice.

Why the return genuinely does not matter

The Guide says it in one line: “This is not a competition to see which team can make the most money in a few weeks.” Ranking, trade count, beating other teams and whether the portfolio makes money are all listed as things we are not evaluated on.

It is a one-to-three-month Treasury bill fund — cash wearing a ticker, earning the short rate while it waits. It is Pile C, it has a written job, and the only reason it looks invested is that the platform files every ETF as an equity.
