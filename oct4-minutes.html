Raised on the call, written down nowhere else

Four things worth knowing before Tuesday.

  1. Coach P leans toward spreading the purchases out.

    Michael, on the call: “He was definitely leaning towards kind of spacing it out, but I think commissions could kind of eat us on that.”

  3. The fill-timing disagreement, and why Federico was not simply wrong.

    Asked when the orders would fill, Federico said: “if you place the trade on Tuesday afternoon, like, by four, the stock will be bought on Wednesday morning, because the stock market is closed at that time.”

    He attached a condition and it was correct for it. The market closes 3:00pm Central, so a 4pm placement really would fill the next morning. What changed afterwards is that the meeting moved to 1pm lunch, which is inside market hours, so the orders fill Tuesday - and nobody went back to the fill question after the time moved. The record should say the condition changed, not that he got it wrong.
