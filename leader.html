The meeting · 18 minutes

You run the clock

0–2 minSay what the chat replies already settled.
2–4 minOne line each, no arguing. On a trade, Dylan goes first.
4–15 minTalk it out, then vote. 3 of 5 passes; 4 of 5 if it's over 10% of the portfolio, a new kind of investment, or a plan change.
