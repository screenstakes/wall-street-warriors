The case against · step 1

Answer five questions

  1. Where did the numbers come from? A fact, or someone's guess?
  2. Was there a real alternative, or did we take the first idea?
  3. Is our best guess too hopeful?
  4. Is the worst case bad enough?
  5. Did anyone disagree, and did we listen?

Your Bear case tab has one column per trade. Column B is a finished example on VOO, so read it first. Fill yours in C, then D. Rows 5 to 8 first: the trade, who pitched it, and what your Limits tab says about it.
