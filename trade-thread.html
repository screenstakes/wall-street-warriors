Wall Street Warriors · how a trade works

One trade, start to finish, in our Google Chat

Every trade gets its own thread. The replies stack up in order. Nobody votes in the chat: we vote by hand at the meeting, and Federico posts the result back in the thread.

Pitch → Fits Laura? → Case against → Vote at the meeting → Result, placed, logged
In the trade's threadIn the main chatIn person

Made-up example with the same week as our meeting pages. JPM is not a real pick.

  1. 1
    Thu Sep 24 · the pitch

    Joe starts a new thread with his trade card

    Main chat · new message, which starts the thread
    J
    Joe Brown7:02 PM

    TRADE CARD: Buy JPM

    How much
    $15,000, 5% of the portfolio
    Why
    Biggest US bank. Fills our Financials slot.
    Laura's goal
    Pile B, growth for the building
    What it's worth
    My value is about 20% above today's price (Valuation tab)
    What makes it wrong
    A recession that drives up loan losses
    Vote
    Wednesday's meeting
    ↳ 5 replies

    Anyone can pitch. One trade, one new message. Everything about this trade after that is a reply in its thread, never a new message.

  2. 2
    By Sun 8 PM · fits Laura?

    Caleb replies: which of Laura's goals it serves

    ↳ Reply in the JPM thread
    C
    Caleb HamelsFri 4:10 PM

    Fits: Pile B growth. Nothing is due until 2033, so a bank's ups and downs are fine for this pile.

    If it serves none of her goals, he sends it back and the trade stops here. Joe's number is already in the card because he pitched it. If someone else pitches, Joe replies here with what the stock is worth.

  3. 3
    By Tue 8 PM · the night before the vote

    Dylan replies with the case against

    ↳ Reply in the JPM thread
    D
    Dylan WroanTue 7:40 PM

    Case against JPM: banks fall hardest in a recession, and this puts our whole Financials slot in one company. 5% on day one leaves no room to add if it drops. The 20% is Joe's estimate, not a fact, and a slightly higher discount rate shrinks it.

    I'd argue for 3% now, and adding later if it holds up.

    Last reply before the vote, on purpose: he can read the pitch and Caleb's answer first. If it's late, the team votes without it.

  4. 4
    Tue 8 PM · Michael

    Michael puts the vote on tomorrow's meeting

    Main chat · new message, not in the thread
    M
    Michael Fitzgerald8:00 PM

    Meeting tomorrow, zero period, library. One line each by 7 a.m.

    1. Vote on Joe's JPM card (read its thread first): 5%, or Dylan's 3%?
    2. Who pitches next week?

    Nobody votes here. Michael just puts it on the meeting's list.

  5. 5
    Wed · zero period

    The vote, in person

    1. Dylan talks first, with the case against.
    2. Joe answers. Everyone else, one line each.
    3. Hands up on 5%. Federico counts out loud: three.
    5% is under 10% of the portfolio, so 3 of 5 passes. Approved.

    Over 10% of the portfolio, a new kind of investment, or a change to the plan would need 4 of 5.

  8. ✓
    Where it ends up

    One thread, plus one line in each sheet

    The threadThe whole story of the decision in one place. Our Oct 23 Trading Notes are built from threads like this.
    FedericoA row on his Log tab
    CalebA row on his Fit check tab
    DylanA column on his Bear case tab
    JoeHis Valuation tab, and the WInS note
