What this is doing

He is reading “Buying Power” off a screen that files a T-bill fund under equities. He is not wrong, he is looking at a number we never explained. Saying so is both true and the fastest way past it.

It explains yesterday in one cause, not six figures

One inflation number moved rate expectations, and that hit the borrowers and the AI names together. The earlier draft of this message had ten numbers in it and read like a defence. The point is not to prove we did the maths, it is that he understands what happened.

It admits the rule was never voted on

This is the part that matters. If he repeats the rule to Dylan or Federico and they have never heard of it, the whole thing falls apart and it looks like we made it up. Saying it first costs nothing and it is the truth: it was written up, published, and treated as settled without ever going to the room.

It also turns a weak position into a strong one. “We have a rule” is a claim he can check. “I found a gap in how we decide things and I am fixing it tomorrow” is a thing a coach wants to hear.
