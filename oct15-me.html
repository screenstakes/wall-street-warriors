“If there is significant gain in NVIDIA, sell it and take profit”

Say it There is no gain. NVDA is down 5.59%, and it was down 3.3% just this morning. There is nothing to take.
  • Be quick and kind about it. He wrote that on Thursday, before any of this, and he is reasoning from a portfolio he has not seen. Correct the fact, do not make it a thing.
  • Then use it, because the instinct behind it is the one worth addressing: only two of our twelve equity lines are up at all, MDT and BKNG. “Take profits” is not available as a strategy on a book that is down.
  • That also answers Coach P's second and third P without having to argue with him. Partials on gains and protect your profits both need profits.

“We should sell the ones that are losing”

Say it That is selling the half of the portfolio that fell, on the day it fell, and paying 0.60% a position to do it. Give me a reason that is not the price and I will put it on the list.
  • Point at the other half. JNJ, BKNG and MDT were all up today and both Treasuries did not move. The book did what it was designed to do. Selling the part that fell is dismantling the design.
  • Then the real argument: we are not scored on this. Read them the line from the Guide — “whether your portfolio makes money during the competition” is on the list of things we are explicitly not evaluated on.
  • Land it: a loss with a written reason scores. A gain with no reason does not. That is the whole competition.
