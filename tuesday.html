How to key it

  • Shares come from Tuesday's price, not from this sheet.

    Pull the quote inside WInS at the moment you key it. Shares = the dollar budget divided by the price, rounded down. Never round up — that is how an order gets cancelled for insufficient cash.

  • Key BIL last.

    Everything else has a fixed quantity. BIL is the only line sized against whatever cash is actually left, so it absorbs the Treasury fills we cannot see until the orders are in.

  • One login, one person typing.

    Michael keys every order. Nobody else is in the account at the same time.

  • Write the note as you go.

    Someone who is not keying types each trading note while the order goes in — Federico, or Dylan if Federico is not there. Notes cannot be edited once saved, and three of them are the whole Oct 23 deliverable.

  • 1pm Central is inside market hours.

    The market is open 8:30am to 3:00pm Central. Orders keyed after the meeting fill Tuesday, not Wednesday morning.
