Before the trade, not after

Write the card first. Then place the trade.

Every serious student fund works this way: the write-up has to exist and be approved before anyone can trade. It means the record can never be wrong later, because there is no later.

About a minute, mostly tapping. You get one block to paste into the chat, and the WInS note is inside it.

Why it matters: Wharton quotes three of these notes back, word for word, in October, and checks them against your real trades. WInS will not let you edit a note once it is saved.

Two things to paste

1. Reply in the trade's thread

2. Federico's log cells

Click the first empty row in column A and paste. This fills the date, ticker and side. Then the shares, price and votes go in D, E and L of that same row — they are listed under the block.

Why not one paste across the whole row? Columns F and G are formulas that work out the dollar value and the weight. Pasting blanks over them would wipe them for good, so the tool stays out of those two cells.
