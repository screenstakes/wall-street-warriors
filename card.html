Wall Street WarriorsTrade card
Before the trade, not after

Write the card first. Then place the trade.

Every serious student fund works this way: the write-up has to exist and be approved before anyone can trade. It means the record can never be wrong later, because there is no later.

This takes about a minute. Most of it is tapping. At the end you get three things to paste: the note for WInS, the card for the chat, and Federico's log row.

Why it matters: Wharton quotes three of these notes back, word for word, in October. WInS will not let you edit a note once it is saved.

Yours to nameThis is the job you are assigning the position. The whole team votes on whether they agree.
