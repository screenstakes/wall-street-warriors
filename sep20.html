The 11 industries — and the 5 we own

Every listed company sits in exactly one of these eleven. It's the standard Wall Street list, called GICS, and it's what the trading site sorts by.

The rule

Five of us means the portfolio must hold a stock in at least five different industries — and keep it that way all season.

Also required

At least one ETF (a fund that holds many stocks at once) by the checkpoint.

Past teams had to name who ran each industry in the final report. That's why you pick one.

The one that trips people up

Mastercard is Financials, not Tech. It feels like a tech company, but in 2023 the card networks were officially moved out of Information Technology and into Financials. So Mastercard does not collide with Microsoft — they count as two different industries, which is exactly what we need.

Same idea with Waste Management: garbage and recycling is Industrials, not Utilities and not Energy.
