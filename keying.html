Nothing. The one thing nobody can see tonight is the fill price, because it will be Thursday's opening price rather than Wednesday's close.

✓The price is not what this page says.

Expected, and not a problem. The budget is the ceiling; the share count is whatever the budget bought at the price on screen, and each card now prints the price its order was keyed at. Use the screen, not this page.

✓The division does not come out even.

It never will. Round DOWN, every time, on every line.

Do this

Never round up. Rounding up is how an order comes back cancelled for insufficient cash, and then you have lost the time as well as the order.

✓The price went up and I can afford fewer shares than the page shows.

Then you buy fewer shares. Nothing else on the sheet moves.
