When it goes wrong

14 things that can happen at the table, most likely first. Every one of them has a single action.

✓The market is closed. Can I even place these?

Yes. StockTrak says so on the screen: orders placed while the market is shut are executed at the next open. That is Thursday at 8:30am Central, and it happens without you — you do not have to be online for it.

Do this

Key all of it now. The one thing you cannot see is the fill price, because it will be Thursday's opening price rather than tonight's close.

✓The price is not what this page says.

Expected, and not a problem. The budget is the ceiling; the share count is whatever the budget buys at the price on screen. Use the screen, not this page.

Do this

Shares = budget divided by the price on screen, rounded DOWN. Key that.

✓The division does not come out even.

It never will. Round DOWN, every time, on every line.

Do this

Never round up. Rounding up is how an order comes back cancelled for insufficient cash, and then you have lost the time as well as the order.

✓The price went up and I can afford fewer shares than the page shows.

Then you buy fewer shares. Nothing else on the sheet moves.

Do this

Do not top it up from another line, and do not round up to reach the number printed here. The leftover dollars stay in cash and BIL absorbs them at the end.

!The order came back INSUFFICIENT CASH.

You rounded up, or there is less cash than you expect because an earlier line cost more than its estimate.

Do this

Drop one share and key it again. If it fails twice, drop one more. Do not move on with the line unplaced.

!The order came back SYSTEM CANCELLED.

That is the position limit, and it should not happen today — the biggest line is VOO at 19.9% and the cap is 25%.

Do this

Screenshot it and stop keying that line. That screenshot finally answers the 25% question we have had open since September. Tell the team, then carry on with the next order.

!I am keying after the close. Does BIL still work the same way?

No, and it is the only thing the timing changes. BIL is last because it absorbs whatever cash is really left — but nothing fills until Thursday's open, so tonight there is no real balance to size it against.

Do this

Key all fourteen tonight, BIL included, at 492. The cushion is about $6,980 — bigger than the plan assumed — so it fits. If something bounces on Thursday morning, shrink BIL then, and never a stock or a bond.

!An order bounced on Thursday morning while I was in class.

Everything queued executes at the open without you. If one fails for insufficient cash, the others still went in — you are not starting over.

Do this

Check Order History at lunch Thursday. Re-key only the failed line, one share smaller. If it was BIL, just size it to the cash that is actually there.

Do this

Still key 492 BIL, not more. The extra stays in cash. A bigger reserve than the team voted for is a decision nobody made, and Sunday may shrink it anyway.
