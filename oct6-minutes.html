What was decided

Where the Oct 4 record cites a timestamp, this one cites Michael's account. There is nothing else to cite.

    Federico's objection: at about $5,000 a name, a 10% gain is $500 — too little to matter. The room agreed to put more into the stocks.

    ✓settledSource: Michael's account

The room decided not to keep those slots for five more names, and to move that money — plus the $6,000 Robinhood freed when it lost the vote — into the nine names already approved by the Oct 3–4 ballot.

    ✓settledSource: Michael's account

  3. 3The weighting is a hybrid of Caleb's Oct 4 proposal.

    GE Vernova and Johnson & Johnson get the most, NVIDIA a step below, the other six an equal share. The exact dollars were left to be worked out.

    The order sheet (wednesday.html) now carries GEV $14,000, JNJ $14,000, NVDA $9,500 and six at $8,500 — $88,500 in all — to be confirmed in the team chat. The shape is the room's decision; the dollars are the sheet's working.

    ✓settledSource: Michael's account

  4. 4Nothing was keyed on Tuesday. The orders went in on Wednesday evening.

In the event the lunch window was missed and the orders were keyed on Wednesday evening instead, after the close, which means they execute at Thursday's open. Nothing else changed.
