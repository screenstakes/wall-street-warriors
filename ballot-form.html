The ballot, paste-ready

Nine questions with the blurb and the options already written. Copy each into a Google Form. About ten minutes.

Settings to use: collect email addresses (so you know who has not answered), one response per person, allow editing after submit — people change their minds and you want the final view, not the first one.

Add one free-text box at the end: “Anything you disagree with, or that I got wrong?” That box is where criterion 4 lives. A ballot where everyone ticks option one is worth nothing as evidence.

Close it Wednesday Oct 21, write the result up the same day, and post it win or lose. The Oct 4 ballot worked because the result got published; this one has to as well.
